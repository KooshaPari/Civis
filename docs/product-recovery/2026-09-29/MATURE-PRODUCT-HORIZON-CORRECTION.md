# Mature product horizon correction — Civis / CivLab

Date: 2026-09-30.
Authority: USER INTENT CORRECTION / RECOVERED USER INTENT.
This document corrects product framing; it does not change the frozen semantic-save implementation candidate.

## Accepted intent

Civis/CivLab is a long-horizon civilization/god-game/warfare mega-simulation.

Recovered user-intent anchors:
- 2026-02-20: decade-scale mega-sim; deeper mathematical formalization; 4X mechanics and scarcity; multiple LOD scales; evolutionary/progressive development from arbitrary starting points toward spacefaring (not yet multi-planetary).
- 2026-09-30: intended synthesis of influences from Cities: Skylines, Empire at War, Manor Lords, DINO/DINOForge and Roblox, combining simulation/visual qualities, warfare and god-game interaction with Civis's own emphasis on emergence and deep physics simulation.
- 2026-08-19 umbrella intent also emphasized deep economy, warfare, nested supply chains and grand-to-granular play across the game/mod family.

The named games are design-source dimensions, not a requirement to clone them.

## Mature ontology directions

The mature product must be able to explain, subject to archaeology/acceptance:
- physical/world substrate;
- people/population/agents;
- settlements/cities/civilizations;
- economy/resources/logistics/scarcity;
- institutions/politics;
- culture/language/religion;
- research/technology/progression;
- diplomacy;
- warfare;
- construction/urban development;
- environment/ecology;
- emergence and causal explanation;
- player/god intervention;
- visualization/rendering;
- creation/modding/extensibility;
- persistence/continuity;
- observability of why outcomes emerged.

This is an ontology/discovery map, not a flattened requirement catalog.

## Current recovery slice

The semantic persistence program is foundational because a deep emergent world cannot be a coherent product if its causal state silently resets across save/restart.

It is **not the definition of Civis**.

The 125 Simulation / 48 WorldState denominator should therefore be evaluated against continuity of the mega-simulation and its causal substrate, not as an end in itself.

## Non-normative prior suggestions

Earlier assistant-proposed feature lists (for example specific covert/deep-state, biometric/constitutional, or other detailed systems) remain ASSISTANT SUGGESTION unless independently supported by user intent/repository evidence. They must not silently enter the accepted mature contract.
